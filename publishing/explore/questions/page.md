Drawloom is just getting started. These are the questions shaping what comes
next.

- **Which AI agents should come next?** Drawloom works with Codex today, and
  it's designed to welcome others.
- **Where else should Drawloom run?** It starts on the Mac. We'd love to bring it
  to more of the places you work.
- **How can Drawloom fit your organisation?** We'd love to work with teams that
  want Drawloom to follow their own rules and systems.
- **What should your workbench help you do?** Drawloom is built for real work.
  Tell us about yours.
