You should be able to rely on the tools you build on. Quality is built into
everything we do.

- **Tested with every change.** Every change to Drawloom runs through more than a
  thousand automated tests.
- **The same standard for every part.** Every implementation of a capability,
  ours or yours, passes the same tests.
- **Tested the way you use it.** We test the app and libraries exactly as you
  install them.
- **Built to recover.** Drawloom is tested against crashes and restarts, so your
  work is safe when the unexpected happens.
- **Trusted by your Mac.** The Drawloom app is signed and notarised by Apple.
