Drawloom is a desktop app you can try today, and a toolkit you can build on.

## Try the app

Drawloom brings your projects, your conversations with an AI agent and the work
it produces into one place. You direct the agent, see what it's doing and approve
what matters.

**You'll need:**

- A Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 14 or later.
- [Codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex), installed and signed in.
  Drawloom is free. It uses your own Codex account.

**To install:**

1. Download Drawloom from the
   [preview release](https://github.com/mafifi/drawloom/releases/tag/v0.0.0-preview.1).
2. Open the download and drag Drawloom into Applications.
3. Open Drawloom.

If you install Codex while Drawloom is open, quit Drawloom and open it again.

**What you can do:**

- **Work in projects.** Each conversation belongs to a project folder on your
  computer.
- **Direct the agent.** Send requests with files and images, and steer or stop
  the work as it runs.
- **Stay in control.** Approve actions yourself, or let Codex's own review
  handle them.
- **See the results.** Open what the agent made, including audio, video, PDFs
  and text.
- **Find past work.** Search, rename and archive your conversations.
- **Add plugins.** Bring in new skills and tools.

This is an early preview, so expect rough edges. If something goes wrong,
please [let us know](https://github.com/mafifi/drawloom/issues).

## Build with Drawloom

Drawloom gives you the 10 capabilities an AI workbench needs: context, tools,
memory, knowledge, evaluation, orchestration, sandbox, observability, agent
integration, and policy and approval. Use them as they are, or replace the ones
you need with your own.

- **[Get started from source.](https://github.com/mafifi/drawloom/blob/main/apps/desktop/README.md)**
  Build and run Drawloom on your own machine.
- **[Replace a capability.](https://github.com/mafifi/drawloom/blob/main/docs/reference/replacing-capabilities.md)**
  Swap in your own implementation and test it with the same tests we use.
- **[Write a plugin.](https://github.com/mafifi/drawloom/blob/main/docs/reference/plugin-packages.md)**
  Plugins use the open Agent Plugins format, with MCP for tools.
- **[Contribute.](https://github.com/mafifi/drawloom/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)**
  Drawloom is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence, and contributions are
  welcome.
