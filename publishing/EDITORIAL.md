# Journal essays

The voice for everything we write, including the journal, is in
[WRITING.md](../WRITING.md). This page adds what is specific to essays, video
scripts, captions and presentations. The visual system belongs in
[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Telling the story

- Write firsthand accounts in the author's voice. Don't invent experiences,
  dialogue, motives or results to make a story more compelling.
- Start with the work, the problem and why it matters. Bring in the technology
  when it helps explain the story.
- Use concrete examples and active verbs. Say "see what the agent did" before
  reaching for "observability".
- Keep technical detail light. Link to it rather than explaining it in the essay.
- In technical figures, use the 10 capability names from the README, each with
  a short plain explanation.

## Keeping it true

- Only describe what really happened. If something is an estimate, or an idea
  you haven't tried, say so once, simply.
- Link to the source when you mention another product.
- Use screenshots to show real work and diagrams to show how things fit. Never
  present a mockup as a working product.
- Keep research notes and plans beside the essay, not in it.
- Drafting and committing are not approval to publish.

## Before review

Read it aloud. Replace words you would not use in conversation. Remove repeated
points. Check that every paragraph helps the reader understand the work, the
lesson or the next question. Keep useful detail; cut the effort needed to read it.
