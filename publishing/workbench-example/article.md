## Generation is one step

Imagine making a short business video. A model can draft a script, suggest
shots and help assemble a cut. But someone still needs to inspect the claims,
compare versions, fix the awkward scene and decide whether the result is ready.
Those decisions are part of the work—not an inconvenience after generation.

The question is not only “can an agent make this?” It is “can a person understand,
direct and trust the process that produces it?” A conversation helps express
intent. A purpose-built workbench makes the developing artifact visible.

## Keep the work visible

Put the brief, intermediate results and review decisions beside the artifact.
Let the agent carry out tasks while the operator inspects and steers the work.
The harness owns the agent loop; the workbench provides the domain-specific
surface for judging its results. Neither needs to pretend to be the other.

That is the infrastructure thesis behind Drawloom: reusable capabilities and
provider integrations underneath purpose-built workbenches. This example
illustrates the idea; it does not demonstrate a finished Drawloom runtime or
describe a real customer engagement.
